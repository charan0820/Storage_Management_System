FROM SCREEN TO SHELF

Build it. Connect it. Watch it.

A cardboard shelf, two ultrasonic sensors, and one ESP32 DevKit.

Full setup guide ↗

01 / Make two guided rows

Each row is 30 cm deep and about 12 cm wide. Mount the sensor at the rear, facing a flat sliding follower behind the products. Move the follower forward when removing products.

The follower makes the reflecting surface repeatable. Free-form stock without a follower is unreliable.

02 / Connect your sensors

ESP32 GPIO mapping
Row / sensorTRIGECHO*
1 / US_012532
2 / US_022633

* Each ECHO via divider: ECHO → 1 kΩ → GPIO → 2 kΩ → GND. Shared 5 V supply and common GND.

03 / Assign & calibrate

Enter the printed product tag on its row. Flash the firmware and use the same Wi-Fi as this laptop. The ESP32 fetches the sensor-to-row-to-product mapping automatically.

Measure actual full and empty distances. Tags are assigned manually; ultrasonic sensors measure distance, not product identity.
