Hardware connections

Three HC-SR04 sensors share 5 V and GND. Never connect a 5 V Echo directly to the ESP32.

ShelfTrigEcho via divider
01GPIO 25GPIO 32
02GPIO 26GPIO 33
03GPIO 27GPIO 34

Each Echo → 1 kΩ → GPIO junction → 2 kΩ → GND. See docs/hardware.md for mounting and calibration.
